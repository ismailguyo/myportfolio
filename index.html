About Me

Building with purpose, not just code.

I care about creating websites that are useful, easy to navigate, responsive on every screen, and professional enough to represent a real business.

I'm Guyo Boru, a web developer based in Kenya. I build modern websites for small businesses, personal brands, and people who want a stronger presence online.

My background is in ICT, and I'm currently pursuing my diploma at Thika Technical Training Institute. Along the way, I've been building practical experience across frontend development, backend technologies, databases, version control, and deployment.

I especially enjoy turning an idea into something people can actually use, from a simple landing page to a complete business website.

I'm still early in my professional journey, but I'm serious about the work I deliver. Every project is an opportunity to learn, improve, and build something better than the last one.
